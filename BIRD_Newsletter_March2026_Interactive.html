59th Governing Council Meeting of BIRD, Lucknow

20 March 2026

The 59th Meeting of the Governing Council was held at BIRD under the Chairmanship of Dr. Shaji K. V. Conducted in hybrid mode, the meeting witnessed participation from senior officials of NABARD, including Goverdhan Singh Rawat and Dinesh Poolakkunnath, along with Joint Directors of BIRD Kolkata and BIRD Mangaluru as special invitees, reflecting ongoing efforts towards academic integration of BIRD training establishments.

The Council deliberated on key agenda items, including a comprehensive review of academic performance for 2025–26 and the consolidated academic plan for 2026–27 across BIRD TEs. The Chairman emphasised the need to institutionalise integration across centres. Select publications including Financial Inclusion in India – 2025: A Trend Report and Climate Lens were unveiled.

Terminology Demystified
Embedded Credit

A form of digital lending wherein credit is seamlessly integrated into non-financial platforms such as e-commerce or agri-value chain applications. It enables users to access instant, context-specific loans at the point of need, based on real-time data-driven assessment. This model enhances convenience, reduces turnaround time, and expands access to formal credit.

Prepared by Shri. Karan Sharma, Manager, BIRD
